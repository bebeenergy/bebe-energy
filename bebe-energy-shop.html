All editions are delivered as high-quality PDF files, formatted for A4 with a dark background and gold typography. They're gorgeous on screen and print beautifully on any home or professional printer.

Yes — for personal use and sharing, absolutely. If you're creating content that directly monetises the quotes (ads, merchandise, commercial projects), please reach out first. The spirit of Bébé Energy™ is generous, not litigious.

Yes — each edition contains the quotes from the previous one, plus more. The 100 is quotes 1–100. The 300 is quotes 1–300. And so on through to the complete 1,000. If you buy the bundle, you have everything.

Almost certainly. The collection started at 1,000 and the energy shows no signs of running out. Bundle buyers will be first to know when new editions drop. Sign up for the newsletter to stay in the loop.
